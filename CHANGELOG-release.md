## 3.2.0 — 2026-11-06

### Added
- Professions: **Show profession levels on screen** writes each profession you
  know with your skill and the maximum of your rank (for example
  "Mining: 63/150"). You can drag it, lock it, resize it and center it.

### Changed
- Fishing options moved to the **Professions** page. Your settings come with
  them.
- The **Professions** page now also loads on retail.
