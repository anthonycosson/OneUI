## 3.8.0 — 2026-11-12

### Added
- Buff tracker: a second frame, **Group / raid buffs**, shown in a group only.
  It lists each buff a class in your group can give you and you lack. Paladin
  blessings and auras count: one per Paladin, with the number missing.

### Changed
- Buff tracker: **Class buffs** now show one clickable icon per class buff you
  can cast (Mark and Thorns, Armor and Intellect…). Shamans and Rogues need
  their weapon buff on every weapon. **Camp buff** is covered by any camp buff,
  or by the class buff matching a camp object in your bags, whatever its rank.
- Buff tracker: each frame can set its **icons per row** and its **growth
  direction**.
- Every resizable frame gets a **Size** slider, to give two frames exactly the
  same size: buff tracker, gathering tracking, profession levels, craft timer,
  raid tool bar and loot roll window.
